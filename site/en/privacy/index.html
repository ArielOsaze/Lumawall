Privacy Policy

LumaWall runs entirely on your own computer. We collect no data at all.

In short: no account, no telemetry, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing sent to us. The only network connection happens when you ask for a wallpaper to be downloaded.

Data we collect

None. LumaWall has no server that receives data from the app, no account system, and sends no usage or crash reports.

Data stored on your computer

Everything is stored locally and is never sent anywhere:

  • %LOCALAPPDATA%\LumaWall\config.json - your settings: the chosen wallpapers, monitor profiles, timer settings and language.
  • %LOCALAPPDATA%\LumaWall\Wallpapers\ - the wallpapers you downloaded or imported.
  • %LOCALAPPDATA%\LumaWall\Thumbnails\ - cached preview images, so the catalogue opens faster.
  • %LOCALAPPDATA%\LumaWall\Logs\ - technical notes to help you report a problem. They contain file names and error messages, not personal data.

Deleting %LOCALAPPDATA%\LumaWall deletes all of it. Uninstalling does not delete it, so your wallpapers and settings survive an update.

Network connections

LumaWall opens a network connection in exactly two situations, and you trigger both:

  • When you press Download on a wallpaper in the catalogue. The request goes straight to that wallpaper provider's address. It carries an ordinary file request, the same as any browser.
  • When you refresh the catalogue, if you set your own feed address in Settings. The app fetches a JSON file from the address you chose.

No request is sent to the LumaWall developers. Nothing is added to those requests beyond what is needed to download the file.

Third-party content

Wallpapers come from their original providers. Their rights and licences follow the source, and are recorded in a .license.txt file next to each download. When you download from a third-party provider, that provider's privacy policy applies to the request.

Children

LumaWall collects no data from anyone, including children. The catalogue has a category filter, and the Mature 18+ category can be hidden in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is what gets updated and the date below changes. Since the app collects nothing, significant changes are not expected.
