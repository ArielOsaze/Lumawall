 My video wallpaper is lagging 

 What should I do to minimize or defeat lagging without lowering video quality? I can play this video in MPC-BE in 4x speed (4x required perfomance) and don't see ANY lagging (only sound dissapears sometimes). In the Wallpaper Engine this video can lag for 1-2 seconds and have many micro-freezes. How can I fix it?

 Wallpaper Engine uses your graphics card to decode the video because this is much more efficient when it comes to power usage while MPC will use your CPU. It is likely that your GPU is hitting some sort of limitation with decoding the video file. You can try to switch to LAV and DirectShow as explained here, it might be more efficient for your special use-case:

 https://help.wallpaperengine.io/en/videos/lav.html 

Alternatively, you can also disable the "Video hardware acceleration" option in the "General" tab of the Wallpaper Engine settings. I do not recommend this because it will put load on your CPU but it might help in your case. Try the LAV solution first, make sure to follow all the steps as explained on the page I linked above.

 The CPU usage is too high for a video wallpaper, I think Windows is unable to use hardware acceleration on your video file for some reason. The fact that you have sound stuttering in MPC is also a pretty good give-away that the file is hitting some hardware or driver threshold. The config file mentions multiple files so I am not sure which one you are having issues with but I would probably try to just re-encode the file(s) with Handbrake. You can very likely improve the file without affecting visual fidelity significantly by re-encoding the file as an .mp4 file with H.264 encoding and a reasonable bitrate. Check out Handbrake here:

 https://handbrake.fr/
