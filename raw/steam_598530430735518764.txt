 High CPU usage for Video Wallpaper 

 Why does Wallpaper Engine have such a high CPU usage playing back a video Wallpaper?

I took a video that is 120fps 4K and when setting it as a wallpaper, Wallpaper Engine uses 45% CPU on my R9 5900x. When I play the same video in VLC, it uses less than 0.8% CPU.

This is even a re-render. I playback the original 240fps video in VLC and CPU is still between 0.6% and 0.8%, But Wallpaper Engine cannot even play it and shows like 2 seconds of it before basically pausing it.

I have change the setting in Wallpaper Engine and no combination really affects the performance of video wallpapers. 

Sure, I can re-render the video at 60fps, but I do not see why that is a solution where normal video playback does not have such an issue. Is this just a limitation of how it renders videos as a background that introduces a ton of overhead? Or is there something I can do to change this?

 Try switching to LAV with DirectShow, it may be that the Windows component that Wallpaper Engine relies on cannot handle your video format (encoder or bitrate would probably the issue) properly and it falls back on to the CPU:

 https://help.wallpaperengine.io/videos/lav.html
