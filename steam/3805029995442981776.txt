 It depends on the wallpaper, mainly the type and its resolution. Right now the app is using 41 Megabytes of system memory on my system on a 4K screen. It's insignificant in the vast majority of cases.

If you change the "Other application full screen" option to "Stop (free memory)" in the performance tab of the settings, Wallpaper Engine will automatically unload itself whenever you launch a full-screen game and restart once you quit your game. That puts the RAM usage to basically 0 while you are playing games.
