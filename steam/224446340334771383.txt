 I was looking to see why Microsoft abandoned DirectShow. To see what the differences are that makes MF superior. Turns out MF doesn't replace DS yet, and for certain use cases DS is still the recommended solution per Microsoft. Wallpaper Engine, needing only to do decoding, would be recommended to use MF per their guidelines.

DS native format support:
 https://msdn.microsoft.com/en-us/library/windows/desktop/dd407173(v=vs.85).aspx 

Edit:
Also, the built in H.264 DirectShow codec wouldn't help us much. It only supports decoding videos up to 1920x1088. All users would have to install codecs to enable 4k support.
