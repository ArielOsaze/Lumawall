 Videos are played through Windows Media Player (kind of simplified but accurate enough). If 4K videos stutter, there is likely an issue with your drivers or some Windows configuration, as all videos are rendered by your graphics card. Make sure you are not using the internal GPU of your Intel processor and that all displays are connected to your Nvidia graphics card (and not the Intel GPU).
