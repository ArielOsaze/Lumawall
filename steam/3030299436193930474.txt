 There is no option to do this right now because it would only look good with very specific videos (basically if the video background is a solid color so it can nicely blend with the rest of the screen). With other videos it would just be kind of pointless since you could clearly see the border and it wouldn't look proper.

We may add more alignment options in the future and then this option would also be added. See the roadmap: https://steamcommunity.com/app/431960/discussions/0/1500126447391526102/ 

I don't know what kind of video you have, but if this gets added then of course you could do it with any video.
